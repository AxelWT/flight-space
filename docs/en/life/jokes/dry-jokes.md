# Dry Jokes

> Warning: may cause groaning and eye-rolling. Proceed with a straight face.

<div style="margin: 40px 0;">

---

**Q:** What do you call a fake noodle?
**A:** An impasta.

---

**Q:** Why don't scientists trust atoms?
**A:** Because they make up everything.

---

**Q:** What do you call a bear with no teeth?
**A:** A gummy bear.

---

**Q:** Why did the scarecrow win an award?
**A:** Because he was outstanding in his field.

---

**Q:** What do you call a fish wearing a bowtie?
**A:** Sofishticated.

---

**Q:** Why don't eggs tell jokes?
**A:** They'd crack each other up.

---

**Q:** I used to play piano by ear, but now I use my hands.

---

**Q:** What's the best thing about Switzerland?
**A:** I don't know, but the flag is a big plus.

---

**Q:** Why did the bicycle fall over?
**A:** Because it was two-tired.

---

**Q:** I told my wife she was drawing her eyebrows too high.
**A:** She looked surprised.

---

**Q:** How do you make holy water?
**A:** You boil the hell out of it.

---

**Q:** Why don't skeletons fight each other?
**A:** They don't have the guts.

---

**Q:** What do you call a guy with a rubber toe?
**A:** Roberto.

---

**Q:** Why did the golfer bring two pairs of pants?
**A:** In case he got a hole in one.

---

**Q:** What's orange and sounds like a parrot?
**A:** A carrot.

---

**Q:** I'm reading a book on anti-gravity.
**A:** It's impossible to put down.

---

**Q:** Why did the math book look so sad?
**A:** Because it had too many problems.

---

**Q:** How do you catch a squirrel?
**A:** Climb a tree and act like a nut.

---

**Q:** What do you call a can opener that doesn't work?
**A:** A can't opener.

---

**Q:** Why did the coffee file a police report?
**A:** It got mugged.

---
