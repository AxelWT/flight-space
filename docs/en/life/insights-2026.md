## Economics

- GDP growth dropping from 8% to 5% may mean nearly 30% of people face employment difficulties — **A small decline in growth rate corresponds to massive individual hardship.**
- When thinking about economics, avoid black-and-white either/or thinking. The better methodology is **weighing pros and cons**. Binary thinking is dangerous because it ignores trade-offs and costs in reality.

## Culture

- There's a saying: **Western society is a horizontal society; Eastern society is a hierarchical society.**
  - The West: Everyone pursues self-transcendence — adventurers risk death to push limits, driven from within.
  - The East: You pursue "rising above others," which is essentially about being better than others — but when the goal is to be better than others, it's exhausting, because people are complex and comparison is endless.

- The trap of pursuing "being above others": The standard is external, relative, and always changing. Pursuing "self-transcendence" has an internal, absolute, self-defined standard. The former is a zero-sum game; the latter is a positive-sum game.

## Science

- **Science is not a stance, not politics, and does not compromise for power** — it is objective facts and laws.
- Following science means using **mathematical logic and quantitative data** to qualitatively and quantitatively describe problems, state facts, and find solutions.
- The core spirit of science: **Falsifiability** — if a theory cannot be falsified, it does not belong to the realm of science.
