Hi, I'm Felix. Like most people, work days are 9-to-6, weekends are for movies and good food, and occasionally I find something new to obsess over.

## What I Do

I write code for a living — Java, distributed systems, and lately some AI and LLM stuff. It's my job but also my interest. Outside of work I tinker with side projects (like this blog). The motto is: have fun while building things.

## Things I Love

- **Photography** — Started taking it seriously a couple of years ago. I love street photography and landscapes. Still very much a beginner, but every time I capture a shot I'm happy with, it makes my day. Slowly learning how to frame the world the way I see it.
- **Sports** — I try to stay active. Nothing beats the feeling of a good sweat followed by a hot shower — it's like a system reboot for the body and mind.
- **Movies & Music** — I'm a big fan of classic movies. I've watched Stephen Chow's films countless times. Music is a daily essential — different moods call for different playlists. Lately I've been collecting instrumental music perfect for coding.
- **Reading** — I read across the board: history, biography, psychology, investing. Not a heavy reader — just a few books a year, but I try to make each one count.

## About This Blog

This is my little patch of the internet — a place to jot down things I've read, thought about, or experienced. Book notes, movie recommendations, random thoughts, and occasional tech experiments. It's more of a personal garden than a serious blog. I plant things I like, and if someone wanders by and finds something interesting, that's a bonus.

Feel free to say hi anytime.
